🍜 Our Story

ONE PIECE Chicken Kobayashi was born from a passion for authentic Japanese cuisine and a dream to bring the bold, savory flavors of Japan to our local community. What started as a small kitchen experiment has grown into a beloved destination for food enthusiasts seeking genuine Japanese Karaage and comfort food.

Our name pays homage to the adventurous spirit of exploration and discovery. Like the journey of finding the ultimate treasure, we embarked on a quest to perfect our recipes, combining traditional Japanese cooking techniques with locally-sourced ingredients to create something truly special.

Every dish we serve is crafted with care, using time-honored methods passed down through generations. From our crispy Karaage chicken to our savory Pork Bites and sweet Graham Bars, each item on our menu tells a story of dedication, quality, and love for great food.
